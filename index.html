Who we are

Welcome to Home Downsize

Let Home Downsize be your trusted partner in simplifying your life and creating a space that works for you.

We specialize in making home downsizing a seamless and stress-free experience. With years of expertise, we help individuals and families transition to smaller, more manageable living spaces with ease. Whether you're an empty nester, a retiree, or simply seeking a more minimalistic lifestyle, our team is here to guide you through every step.

From decluttering and organizing to moving and space planning, we offer personalized services tailored to your needs. We understand downsizing can be emotional and overwhelming, which is why we approach every project with care, respect, and attention to detail.

What we do

Services tailored to your move

Everything you need to downsize with confidence — from the first sorted box to a home that's ready to sell.

Decluttering

We review each room, sort items into keep, donate, sell and discard, then organise what stays for easy access — leaving you with a calmer, more functional home.

  • Room-by-room assessment
  • Sorting & decision-making
  • Organisation & disposal

Moving, Packing & Unpacking

Essential support for relocation, whether you're downsizing or upgrading. We reduce the stress and physical burden of moving with hands-on help at every step.

Estate Cleaning

Thorough cleaning, organising and clearing of a property after a significant life event — sorting, decluttering and preparing the home for sale or future use.

Home Sale Preparation

Getting a property ready to attract buyers and secure the best price — a blend of cleaning, repairs, staging and curb-appeal enhancements to show the home at its best.
